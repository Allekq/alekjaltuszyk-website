## 2. What the app does

Logic Gates is a visual digital-logic editor and simulator for exploring circuits. Its features include touch and Pencil wiring, projects and folders, workspace tabs, reusable circuit blocks, live inspection inside blocks, clocks and flip-flops, edit/run modes, propagation steps, history, waveforms, truth tables and Karnaugh-map minimization. Builds that provide file actions and Trash also support user-directed project export/import and recoverable deletion. Feature availability and behavior can differ between builds and devices.

Simulation is a software model for learning, experimentation and design exploration. **It does not promise hardware-accurate timing, electrical behavior or suitability for safety-critical engineering.** Independently verify results before relying on them outside the app. Do not use the app as the sole basis for decisions where an incorrect result could cause injury or damage.
