## Website Use

The current page is promotional and informational. It describes a product direction for a coming-soon iPhone app, not a fully launched public service.

## Route And Safety Expectations

Take Me Somewhere is intended to support local discovery, not replace personal judgment, official signage, traffic laws, or situational awareness. Users are responsible for deciding whether a route, destination, or outing is safe and appropriate.

The app should use map and routing APIs for route geometry, distance, and ETA. Product copy should not be read as a promise that AI invents or verifies directions.

## Availability

Features, launch timing, supported travel modes, and availability may change. Walking-first discovery is the initial product direction; riding and driving modes may be explored later.
