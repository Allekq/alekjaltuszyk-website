# Delete your AudioChoices data

AudioChoices is an iPhone and Android app published by **Alek Jałtuszyk**.

There is no account here — no name, no email address, no password, no sign-in. Detailed listening progress, story choices and endings stay on your device. Optional yes-or-no story milestone reports are described in the Privacy Policy. What is
held on a server is a small set of records keyed to an pseudonymous installation ID, and there is a
control in the app that erases the deletable part of it.

## In the app — one tap

Open **Settings**, expand **Privacy & Legal**, and tap **Delete my data from our server**.

It deletes the deletable server-side records held against your installation's pseudonymous ID, without
you having to describe or match anything.

Data on the device is separate. The **clear data on this device** control sits beside it, and
uninstalling generally removes app-container data, subject to platform backups. iOS Keychain identity can survive reinstall. Clearing removes the listener age and range but retains the limited preferences described in the Privacy Policy. **One thing the clear-data control deliberately keeps:**
the yes-or-no answer about whether your app store says an age check is required where you are. That
stays, so that clearing data is never a way around the content limit it sets, and only your app
store saying otherwise removes it — uninstall/restore behavior is controlled by the platform and is not universal identity erasure.
One thing neither control can remove: if your phone shared an **age range** with the app, that
range belongs to your phone rather than to this app, so it is still there to be shared again if you
ask the app to check. Section 14 of the Privacy Policy sets this out in full.

## By email

Your pseudonymous ID is shown at the top of **Settings → Privacy & Legal**, with a **Copy** button
next to it. Email **audiochoicesaudiobooks@gmail.com** with the subject **"Delete my data"** and paste it in.

Save the ID before uninstalling. If it is lost, explain what proportionate evidence you can provide. We will explain if records cannot be located or control verified; a nonunique name or unnecessary identity document is not a substitute for a missing link.

Requests are answered within one month. For a complex request, we may extend by up to two further months and tell you why within the first month.

## What is deleted

Your star ratings and votes, and the other server-side records held against your ID that are not
covered by the exceptions below. You will be told plainly which records were kept.

## What is kept, and why

**Purchase, subscription and transaction records.** Needed to give you the access you paid for, and
to meet accounting and tax record-keeping obligations under Polish law. A purchase can be restored
years later, so these are not deleted on request.

**A minimal record that this installation used its free unlocks.** Without it, deleting data would
reset an installation's free unlocks and could be repeated indefinitely. Kept on legitimate interests under
Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: the ID, which books its free unlocks were spent on, and the hour the allowance
started.

**The pseudonymous account record itself**, because the records above still need that ID to preserve
paid access, process refunds and prevent abuse.

**Per-book milestone totals** — running counts of how many times a book was started or finished,
with no ID attached. The current aggregate counter table does not attach your installation ID to each contribution, so it cannot identify and subtract your individual reports. Authenticated requests, logs and any older per-installation records are separate; this statement does not establish their erasure.

**Operational logs** at Google and Cloudflare, for the applicable retention periods. Older Google Analytics records have separate provider request/deletion routes; deactivating measurement in a newer build does not erase them. Contact support about these records.

**A one-way hash of your ID and the request time, in a separate erasure log.** This remains pseudonymous information: an ID already in hand can be checked against it. It supports re-erasure if a database restore brings back old records. Recovery copies and their retention are separate from live deletion; section 15 explains the earlier seven-day recovery commitment and the need to verify actual configuration. Restored records must have applicable erasure requests reapplied before reuse; the log alone does not prove every restore has done this.

## Two things people expect this to do, and it does not

**Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription.** Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google
and must be cancelled there. See the
[Terms of Use](https://alekjaltuszyk.xyz/apps/AudioBookChoices/terms-of-use/).

**Deleting the app does not delete the server-side records.** Use one of the two routes above.
