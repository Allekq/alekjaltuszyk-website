## 3. Information We Handle

### On-Device App Data

Audio Book Choices stores the following only on your device:

- your progress through a story, including which choices you made and where you are in a book
- which books you have unlocked
- app settings and preferences, such as your input method (tap or head-nod) and playback options

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party. Deleting the app removes this local data, subject to normal iOS and Android backup and restore behavior.

### Headphone Motion Data (iOS Head-Nod Feature, Optional)

On iOS, Audio Book Choices offers an optional feature that lets you answer Yes or No by nodding or shaking your head while wearing supported headphones such as AirPods. When this feature is enabled, the app reads headphone motion data from the operating system to detect the gesture.

This motion data is used only in the moment to recognize your gesture. It is processed entirely on your device, is never stored, and is never transmitted off the device. If you do not enable the feature, or your device or headphones do not support it, no motion data is used. You can always make choices by tapping instead.

### Support And Contact Information

If you email support, we may receive your email address, your message, and any details you choose to include, such as device model, OS version, and app version. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information in support messages unless it is necessary for your request.

### Website Technical Information

The Audio Book Choices website pages are static and informational. Ordinary hosting and security infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, referring URL, timestamps, and server logs to deliver and secure the site.

### App Store And Platform Information

If you download or update Audio Book Choices through the Apple App Store or Google Play, those platforms may process account, download, purchase, crash, diagnostics, review, and device information under their own terms and privacy policies. Audio Book Choices does not receive full payment-card numbers from these platforms.
