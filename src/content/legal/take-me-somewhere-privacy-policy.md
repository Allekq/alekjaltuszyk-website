This is an early placeholder privacy policy for the Take Me Somewhere website and planned iPhone app. It will be updated before any broader public launch if the app's data handling changes.

## Current Website

The current Take Me Somewhere page is informational. It does not include accounts, forms, payments, or app-side route generation.

Ordinary hosting and browser infrastructure may process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, and timestamps to deliver the website and keep it reliable.

## Planned App Direction

Take Me Somewhere is intended to help with time-boxed local outings. The app may use device location, map data, and routing APIs to suggest nearby routes, destinations, or compass-guided outings.

Route truth, ETA, distance, and geometry are intended to come from map and routing systems, not invented directions. Any recommendation or intent assistance should support the user's choice of outing, not replace grounded route data.

## Location And Route Data

The app is expected to request location permission only when needed for local route discovery. Exact collection, retention, and sharing details will be finalized in this policy before launch.
