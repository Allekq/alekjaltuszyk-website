Voice of Self uses managed AI to create reflections, summaries, notes, relics, milestones, closing messages and live questions from entries and transcripts you choose to submit. A request may include the current entry or transcript, live transcript excerpts and selected past local context such as summaries, notes, relics or milestones.

Voice of Self is intended for adults aged 18 and over. This eligibility rule does not verify age or replace the separate choice needed for managed processing of sensitive journal information.

Managed AI requires an active subscription, trial, entitlement or access grant linked to a backend identity. Older releases use signed-in accounts. The prepared Kotlin/KMP release uses Firebase anonymous authentication: a persistent pseudonymous installation/session identity without asking you to sign in. It can be linked to backend access, usage and RevenueCat subscription records; it is personal data. Request content passes through the Voice of Self Firebase backend to the configured AI provider. Routes can use Amazon Bedrock / AWS or Groq. Restoring a purchase does not erase data under an older identity.

The backend is designed not to keep transcript text, selected context, prompts or AI output as persistent readable journal records after a normal request completes. Provider retention and technical logs are separate from those journal records. Bedrock retention depends on the model, regional retention configuration and invocation logging; Groq has its own reliability/abuse-monitoring exceptions unless the relevant zero-retention controls are enabled. A general provider default or a disabled logging option alone does not establish every retention setting. Earlier zero-retention commitments are not withdrawn for requests made under them; a route must be verified against the applicable commitment before activation.

Raw audio is not sent to the managed AI backend. Older iOS releases can use Apple speech/transcription services that may involve Apple processing beyond the device. Newer on-device transcription releases process recordings locally. The Privacy Policy describes local storage, retained migration originals, exports and backups; local processing does not itself mean every copy has been encrypted or erased.

Limited identity, subscription, security, diagnostic, performance and usage/accounting metadata is retained, including token counts, workflow labels, timestamps, duration and derived cost. Voice of Self does not use journal content, recordings, transcripts or AI outputs for advertising, custom audiences or training its own models. It does not create voiceprints or biometric identity templates to identify or verify people.

Journal content can include health, religious, political, sexual or other sensitive information. Where explicit consent is required, the AI choice must be separate from general Terms/Privacy acknowledgement and cover the content, purpose and recipients described here. Accepting documents or granting microphone/speech permission alone is insufficient. Do not submit another person's sensitive information without an appropriate basis.

In releases with the Managed AI choice in Settings > Legal, the choice starts off and local journal access does not require enabling it. Your explicit choice covers submitted entries, transcripts, live excerpts and selected past context sent through Firebase to the described providers. You can withdraw in Settings > Legal even while offline. Withdrawal on that installation stops new requests, retries and queued/resumed AI work and prevents older replies from being applied. A request already received by the backend or provider may finish; withdrawal cannot recall content already sent. Enabling again or accepting materially changed consent copy requires a new explicit choice. Restoring app settings does not restore an AI opt-in. Local journals, export and purchase recovery remain available.

An older release without the control does not acquire it from this notice. Stop submitting requests and contact voiceofselfapp@gmail.com for withdrawal or rights assistance, identifying the older release/account where possible. Withdrawal does not itself delete earlier records or cancel a store subscription; any continuing retention requires its own applicable legal basis. Restoring a purchase or creating a new Support ID does not identify or erase an older account.

AI output can be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated or unsuitable. Voice of Self is a self-reflection tool, not healthcare, therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis monitoring, emergency response, legal advice, financial advice or another professional service.
