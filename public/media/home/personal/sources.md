# Personal portfolio media

The work photograph and front-lever recording were supplied by the owner for
the website. They are genuine camera media, with no generated subject or scene.

- `work-evening-640.webp` and `work-evening-1280.webp`: resized WebP exports of
  the supplied evening work photograph. The original is not published.
- `front-lever-hold.webp`: a still from the supplied recording.
- `front-lever.mp4`: a short, resized H.264 extract of the same recording.
  Audio and auxiliary tracks are omitted. It is only fetched near its section
  when motion is enabled and data-saving mode is off.

Photo metadata and source recording metadata are not retained in the web
exports. The source files, extra portraits and original recording stay outside
the public repository. Captions describe only what the media shows.
