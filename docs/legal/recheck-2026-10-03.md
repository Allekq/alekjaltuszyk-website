## Primary authorities checked again

- [EDPB lawful processing and consent](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/process-personal-data-lawfully_en): optional purposes, free choice and withdrawal.
- [Apple privacy and tracking](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/user-privacy-and-data-use/): identifiers other than IDFA can still be used for tracking; a missing IDFA does not decide the classification.
- [FTC COPPA FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions): persistent identifiers can be personal information; internal-operations exceptions are conditional.
- [UOKiK unfair clauses](https://www.uokik.gov.pl/public/niedozwolone-klauzule): consumer exclusions of contractual responsibility can be unfair; a generic savings sentence does not establish enforceability.
- [UOKiK digital-content withdrawal exceptions](https://prawakonsumenta.uokik.gov.pl/prawo-odstapienia-od-umowy/wylaczenia-prawa-do-odstapienia/): prior express consent, acknowledgement and required confirmation are conditions; starting a download alone is insufficient.
- [AWS model/region retention](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/data-retention.html) and [Groq data controls](https://console.groq.com/docs/your-data): provider defaults and a logging switch do not verify the actual managed route.
- [Firestore TTL](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/ttl): expiry timestamps and actual asynchronous deletion are distinct; deployed policy and older records need verification.
- [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement): hosting has technical information processing even for a static site.

### OverLit — close deletion races and verify the retained ad setup

**OL-1: a backend deletion barrier.** Own this in
`OverLit-app/firebase/functions/src/api/deleteIdentity.ts` and the writers under
`api/` and `infra/`. Establish durable deletion state before removing user rows.
Every transaction that creates or changes per-user state must read and enforce
that state in the same transaction as its write. A check at handler entry alone
does not close a race. Include `submitRun`, submission-ledger completion writes,
profile creation, rate counters, nickname fan-out, rating updates/refreshes and
nontransactional fallback paths. Client rules do not protect Admin SDK writers.

Cleanup must resume after partial failure, remain idempotent and report success
only after its defined scope is complete. Keep a minimal protected suppression
record separate from erased profile content. Document its purpose, fields,
access and retention; a hashed UID is still potentially personal data. Choose a
retention limit only after proving how expired/replayed tokens, old clients,
queued requests, jobs and restored backups are prevented from recreating data.
Do not simply expire the barrier and reopen the same UID. The app must reject
late results, clear its relevant cached board/profile state, and avoid
automatically creating a replacement identity as part of deletion.

Acceptance evidence: deterministic emulator tests pause each writer before and
after its transaction read, race deletion against it, retry transactions, replay
requests, interrupt/retry cleanup and exercise nickname fan-out/rating refresh.
No deleted UID can recreate its profile, ledger or entries through any tested
writer. Unrelated players remain untouched. An actual deployed-version check
and synthetic production smoke test are required separately.
