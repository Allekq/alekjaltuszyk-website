## Statutory findings and distinctions

**Polish establishment applies across this review.** The controller's confirmed Polish business establishment means GDPR Article3(1) applies to processing in that establishment's activities regardless of the person's nationality/location. A Korean or Indian location is not an escape from GDPR, and UMP's EEA presentation geography is not equivalent to GDPR scope. For Article6(1)(a) consent-based information-society services offered directly to children, Article8's age threshold matters; Polish UODO guidance uses16. Therefore13/14/15 must not simply self-authorize such purposes under the Polish/default threshold, while16 can cross that particular threshold. Other valid legal bases require their own necessity/fairness assessment; this is not a requirement for guardian consent to every operational data use. [Final EDPB territorial guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-32018-territorial-scope-gdpr-article-3-version_en), [UODO guidance](https://www.uodo.gov.pl/pl/493/2261).

**Korea privacy:** PIPA Article22-2 requires verified legal-representative consent for under14 **when consent is required**. Thus13 differs from14/15/16. It does not create a general minimum age14 for games or mandate guardian consent for every processing operation regardless of legal basis. Foreign-operator applicability, disclosure, transfers and rights remain relevant. Confirm bases for operational UID/rate-limit data and third-party ad processing; a nonpersonalized setting is not an exemption. [PIPA Art22-2 official legislative text](https://elaw.klri.re.kr/eng_service/lawTwoView.do?hseq=62389), [PIPC foreign operator guidance announcement](https://pipc.go.kr/eng/user/ltn/new/noticeDetail.do?bbsId=BBSMSTR_000000000001&nttId=2488).

**Korea game-membership/overuse rules:** Do not automatically require real-name verification, parent membership consent or hourly warnings for this mobile game. Enforcement Decree Art8-3(1) scopes preventive measures to specified devices and excludes terminals using the same type of OS as mobile communications terminals; Art8-3(2) includes further SME exemptions. The iOS/Android distribution described appears outside that device scope. Confirm no separate desktop game distribution before relying on that conclusion. PIPA and content ratings still apply independently. [Official current decree text, Art8-3](https://elaw.klri.re.kr/eng_service/lawTwoView.do?hseq=28798).

**India privacy commencement:** Official GSR843(E) puts Act sections7–10, including child-data section9, in the tranche commencing eighteen months after Gazette publication. Therefore section9 is not in force on2026-10-04. Gazette issue is dated13Nov2025; its digital identifier is14Nov2025. Preserve the operative “eighteen months from publication” language and confirm the scheduled calendar deadline before operational automation. Do not cite future section9 as a current violation or assume it removes existing privacy duties until then. Plan under18/VPC/targeted-ad restrictions with statutory exceptions assessed. [Official commencement notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf).

**India game classification:** OverLit's documented fixed digital purchase, no money-convertible rewards, no paid random item or trade system (`Docs/store/console-values-to-set.md:174–188`) supports a working classification as an online social game rather than prohibited money gaming; validate actual shipped mechanics/rewards/marketing. A score competition alone is not evidence of regulated e-sport registration. The Act's money-game definition turns on payment/stakes and expectation of monetary/other enrichment; social games can charge access fees. Advertising promoting prohibited money games is separately addressed in section6: obtain actual AdMob category blocking/creative evidence, including adult users. [PROG Act definitions and s6](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/08/4f673438a686e3fa81dd2d277b445f42.pdf).

Rules2026 commenced1May2026. Rules8/12 make determination/registration conditional on notified categories, authority directions or e-sport status; not all social games automatically need a certificate. Rule20 does require a functioning grievance mechanism for social-game providers. Rules16/17 contemplate additional directions for contact personnel and Indian data retention; their existence does not alone prove every OverLit record must now be localised. Obtain applicable notifications/orders and actual support operation evidence; a published email alone does not establish functioning redressal. Do not claim official determination/registration without evidence. [Final Rules2026, rr8,12,15–20](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/04/7e0b02d37fd07f81fa48578a9996aa85.pdf).

### Retention and current remediation plan addendum

Read `Docs/reviews/2026-10-04-privacy-remediation.md` after the initial source pass: it now records the SDK mismatch/Families correction and proposes `identityDeletions/{uid}`, transactional barriers, a scheduled pending-cleanup sweep and no automatic expiry. Those are plans or concurrent fixes, not independently tested results in this report.

The permanent-minimal-UID proposal needs a necessity argument beyond “old clients can retry forever.” Firebase ID tokens last approximately one hour and deleting the Auth user invalidates refresh capability; current `api/shared.ts:54–59` only takes callable-auth UID, so in-flight/unexpired-token races still matter. A client retaining an old UID is not itself proof it can authenticate that UID indefinitely. [Firebase session lifecycle](https://firebase.google.com/docs/auth/admin/manage-sessions). The retention review should bound actual deployed callable duration, token verification/revocation behavior, jobs/retries, backups/restore horizons, and ability to recreate/import that same UID. An erasure-suppression record remains personal data even if hashed. Keep automatic expiry disabled until a safe removal condition is demonstrated, but do not present indefinite retention as legally settled. Whether a bounded barrier plus enforced Auth/deletion-generation validation and restore suppression could achieve the purpose with less retention is an explicit release decision.

The proposed scheduled sweep is a new lifecycle and must enter the writer inventory: it may update retry/status timestamps only, never reopen admission; COMPLETE must not regress to PENDING on late failure. Test overlapping sweeps/deletions, starvation, retry caps/backoff, restored rows for a COMPLETE marker, index availability and fail-closed public-read reopening. Admin restores or migration tools can bypass normal barrier helpers, so they require a runbook and acceptance test rather than a blanket guarantee.
