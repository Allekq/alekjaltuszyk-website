## Concrete wording to take into the next legal synchronization

These are **draft replacements**, not activated terms or proof that the proposed behavior
has shipped. They need the corresponding implementation/configuration evidence and a
coordinated version decision. Do not publish optimistic controls that remain unimplemented.

**OverLit diagnostics, replacing the “only on crash / never combined” assurances:**

> When crash reporting is enabled, Firebase Crashlytics can process crash diagnostics,
> installation and session identifiers, and session/stability information. Where Firebase
> Analytics is also enabled, its event names and parameters can be included as crash
> breadcrumbs. Processing is not limited to moments when the app crashes. These records
> are separate from your public leaderboard entries.

Describe the tested age/legal/withdrawal/queued-report conditions in the adjacent paragraph.
Name device-associated attribution fields separately from Apple's aggregate attribution;
do not describe Tenjin as aggregate-only simply because ATT or IDFA is unavailable.

**Voice scope, before revising account/deletion sections:**

> Older releases support signed-in accounts and may use Apple speech-recognition services.
> Newer releases use a pseudonymous installation or session identifier rather than asking
> you to sign in. Some upgraded installations retain an older identity; other releases
> migrate to a new backend identity. The data and deletion procedures that apply depend
> on the release and identity involved. Restoring a purchase does not by itself erase old
> backend records or transfer every journal record.

Add exact tested deletion/request paths and narrow, evidenced retention exceptions. Explain
KMP retained migration originals and local transcription separately; preserve the cloud-speech
caveat for older Swift releases. Keep hosted AI-use, fallback consent copy and public/bundled
policy aligned. A feature-scope masthead does not repair past inaccurate processing notices.

**AudioChoices tracking explanation:**

> This build does not access Apple's advertising identifier. Other identifiers and
> advertising-measurement integrations must also be considered when determining whether
> Apple's tracking permission is required; absence of IDFA alone does not establish that
> processing is nontracking.

Replace this explanatory draft with the actual verified data-flow description before
publishing. Repair age/consent enforcement rather than weakening an existing protective
promise. Withdrawals and changing age must affect the central collection permission.

**Consumer clauses for counsel to adapt across Terms:**

> These terms do not limit your mandatory consumer rights, remedies for failure to supply
> or lack of conformity, applicable withdrawal rights, or data-protection rights. A
> liability cap or indemnity in these terms does not apply to consumer claims where it
> would restrict those protections or to liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

Prefer expressly limiting commercial-use caps to non-consumers. Remove Take Me Somewhere's
blanket consumer indemnity for route decisions and preserve responsibility for the developer's
own conduct. Explain valid reasons and notice/remedies for material reductions of paid services;
Apple/Google refund routes do not replace the developer's statutory obligations. Do not assert
that downloading alone waives withdrawal, or that store seller roles are identical everywhere.

## Common solo-developer mistakes and practical controls

| Mistake | Control and authority |
| --- | --- |
| Treating pseudonymous IDs as anonymous, or SDK defaults as no collection | Maintain an actual data-flow inventory including identifiers, logs and linked SDK automatic behavior. [EDPB guide](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/be-compliant_en). |
| Treating legal acceptance, an OS permission or contextual-ad eligibility as consent for every purpose | Separate optional purposes and test refuse/revoke/relaunch. [EDPB consent guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sites/default/files/files/file1/edpb_guidelines_202005_consent_en.pdf), [Google Consent Mode](https://developers.google.com/tag-platform/security/guides/app-consent). |
| Assuming “no IDFA” means “no tracking” | Audit company-to-company data linking and attribution, including dashboard exports and other IDs. [Apple privacy/tracking](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/user-privacy-and-data-use/). |
| Assuming “13+” or no name prevents child-data duties | Account for actual platform-known age, operational-ID exceptions and optional ad measurement separately. [FTC COPPA FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions). |
| Promising instant/universal deletion from a single client function | Verify every writer, cache, log, backup and processor; supply a workable identification/request route. [Google deletion requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en), [Firestore TTL](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/ttl). |
| Assuming an individual store account is exempt from business duties | Verify trader status and correct public contact details. Apple permits an individual trader to provide an address or PO box for its display; that does not decide every Polish consumer-law address duty. [Apple DSA trader requirements](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-compliance-information/manage-european-union-digital-services-act-trader-requirements/), [UOKiK distance-contract information](https://prawakonsumenta.uokik.gov.pl/prawo-do-informacji/sprzedaz-poza-lokalem-i-na-odleglosc/). |
| Using a strong waiver as the main risk control | Preserve mandatory remedies and have actual consumer clauses reviewed. [EU unfair terms](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/consumer-protection-law/consumer-contract-law/unfair-contract-terms-directive_en), [digital-content Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32019L0770). |
| Having no incident clock or rights-request process | Keep a private request/incident register, access controls and escalation contact. Relevant breaches require regulator notification without undue delay, where feasible within 72 hours; document the risk decision and notify affected people where required. [UODO](https://uodo.gov.pl/pl/525/2584). |

A small operator should keep private processing/retention and provider-contract records,
assess high-risk sensitive processing, and retain actual release/console evidence. Do not
store personal support requests, secrets or production payloads in this public repo.
Use the shared review receipt for every affected change; retain the receipt in the task/PR,
not as a rubber-stamped assertion that the app is compliant.

## Personal exposure and owner decisions

Terms and skills cannot give an individual limited liability. If operating as a Polish sole
proprietor, business obligations can reach personal assets.
[Polish government guidance](https://biznes.gov.pl/pl/portal/00347).
An infringement alone does not automatically establish a GDPR damages award: infringement,
damage and causation are required, though non-material damage has no minimum seriousness
threshold. Regulatory enforcement is separate.
[CJEU, C-300/21](https://curia.europa.eu/jcms/upload/docs/application/pdf/2023-05/cp230072en.pdf).

For asset protection, obtain a focused Polish professional assessment of operating through
a suitable company, store/vendor/IP transfers, director responsibilities and insurance that
covers the actual cyber/privacy/media activities and defense costs. A change of entity does
not erase historical acts or transfer existing obligations automatically. No company or
insurance arrangement was created or represented as an absolute shield.

Before asserting readiness, the owner must supply or verify: actual territories and operator
status; released build/version/commit; store privacy/age/trader declarations; active consent
messages and Analytics/Ads/Tenjin integrations; provider agreements and transfers; cloud
retention/backups/deletion/legacy endpoints; AI zero-retention/logging settings; and the real
support deletion procedure. These are evidence gaps, not optional legal waivers.
